# Korrin pitch film: design truth

Brand values come from the Korrin product's dark theme (`src/app/globals.css`). The application to video is adapted for scale.

- Canvas `#0b0c0e`, raised surface `#131416`, elevated `#1a1b1d`
- Ink `#f2f2f2`, secondary `#cececf`, muted `#8a8b8d`
- Hairlines `rgba(255,255,255,0.09)`; structural rules at 2px for video
- Bid seafoam `#4fb08f`, ask blue `#6b9cf0`. These are data colours: bids, asks and confirmed states
- Accent for focus and selection: `#3b82f6`, used sparingly
- Type: Manrope (variable, embedded, weights 400–500) for headlines and copy. JetBrains Mono (bundled) for figures, ticks, hashes and order IDs
- Headlines are light: weight 400–500 with tight tracking. No heavy display weights
- Do: real figures only, tabular mono numbers, hairline structure, restrained glow
- Don't: neon, gradient text, purple-blue washes, invented metrics, rainbow cards
